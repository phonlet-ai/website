Free for non-commercial use

Personal projects, study, research with no commercial aim, charities, schools, public research and government need no licence (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0). A product you sell, a device you ship or a service your company runs needs a commercial licence.

How the licence works

The brief opens in your own email app, addressed to contact@phonletai.com. This website does not submit your details or subscribe you to anything.
